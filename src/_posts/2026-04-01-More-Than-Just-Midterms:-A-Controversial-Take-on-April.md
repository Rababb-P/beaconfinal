**When you hear the word April, what comes to mind?** The seemingly endless school days following March Break? The dread of the numerous tests and assignments that always come before midterms? The deluge of rain pouring down from the sky? Needless to say, April isn’t exactly a popular month for high schoolers. That’s why I’m here with an unpopular opinion: April is definitely not the worst month, and it might even be the most important one, especially when it comes to the long-term health of our planet. And no, I don’t just love the month because of my birthday, but because of Earth Month.

  

What is Earth Month, you ask? Well, April is Earth Month. Earth Day occurs on April 22nd every year, but the entire month of April is recognized as Earth Month, a time to focus on protecting the environment, reducing our greenhouse gas emissions, and generally taking care of the only planet we call home. Our way of life is not sustainable. If everyone in the world lived like the average Canadian, we would need over five Earths to get the amount of resources we use yearly without triggering catastrophic environmental consequences*  . Every day is a great day to start taking care of our planet, but if you need a little extra motivation, the Martingrove Environmental Club has a challenge for you. Yes, you!
