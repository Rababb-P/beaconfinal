**It hit me around this time last year when my friend was telling me about how he usually accompanied his little brother trick-or-treating, but, for the very first time, he was refused.** We've simply aged out of trick or treating. When you're around the same height as the parents handing out candy, I think it's time to hang up the candy bag for good. But this doesn't have to mean the end of Halloween, so here are a few ways you can have just as much fun sans free sweets.  

  
  **Have a Halloween movie marathon:**  

Hocus Pocus, Beetlejuice, Coraline, and more are all great movies to binge watch. Get your friends together, carve pumpkins, and talk with any spooky classic playing in the background and you've got a cozy way to spend the night.   

  
**Go to Halloween Haunt at Canada's Wonderland:**  

One of the best ways to still get that bewitching Halloween feeling is by going to Halloween Haunt. With eerie mazes, scare zones, and terrifying live entertainment it's the perfect way to get spooked and scream in public without the usual glares.  

  
**Dress up anyway:**  

What did Cady Heron from Mean Girls say, something about Halloween being the one day to dress up and no one “say[ing] anything about it?” Sounds about right. Dress up! Go all out with the makeup and costume design and deciding who to be, because it's the one day of the year everyone else is doing it. Halloween is the best excuse to be over the top.  

  
Getting older and no longer trick-or-treating doesn't mean becoming boring. It just means finding new ways to spend the thirty-first. Whether you're eating candy on the couch or screaming through jump scares, Halloween is still yours to celebrate.
