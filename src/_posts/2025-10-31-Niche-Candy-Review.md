**The candy scene is boring.** I’m not even just talking about Halloween. 24/7/365; people are under the influence of Big Candy™, and I don’t know if anybody realizes that there’s more to life than the mainstream. So, here are some candies of the more niche variety for you to look into this Halloween. Today's the first day of the rest of our lives.  

  
I really, really, really don't expect anybody in our generation to have tried one of these; our declining attention spans wouldn’t allow us to spend the **17 minutes necessary¹** to consume one of them. Of course, I’m talking about the historical artifacts that are Werther’s Original. Established in 1909.  You’d think that this candy-for-the-elderly would predate much of its fanbase, yet I’ve never witnessed anybody from the 18th century or later pop one of these down. The eating experience may be exceptionally arduous on our mouths’ part, but Werther’s Original has a special hominess to it. The caramel flavour is passionate and heartfelt, and it makes me conjure up thoughts of unconditional love.  

  
¹ *While writing this article I downed three Werther’s Originals. On average, it took 16 minutes and 44 seconds to finish one.*  

  
As I eat one, I feel like somebody is smiling at me. At this time, I am also smiling. Even the candy is smiling back at me. And if young trick-or-treaters don’t have the patience to endure the lengthy Werther’s Original experience, they’ll probably have a smile on their face nonetheless—that is unless somebody hands them an easily ingestible box of Smarties.  

  
Unfortunately, for the everyday trick-or-treater, peppermint Aeros are almost exclusively sold as full size bars. However, it might be worth persuading the wealthiest household in your neighbourhood to make a virtuous investment in them this Halloween, because they're simply fantastic. They’re shelled with a thin coating that fortifies a bubbly structure of minted chocolate. It’s simple, but it’s genius, and it’s still everything we know and love about Aero, but even better. The abnormal green themes work nicely for Halloween too—definitely candy for a monster! Speaking of which, the real monsters in our lives who don’t enjoy mint chocolate probably won’t find themselves reaching for one of these all that often. It’s really frightening that they won’t be able to appreciate the delight of one; I proudly do not identify with the mint-disliking niche.  

  
I love it when things cut straight to the chase. *Popeye Candy Sticks* do exactly that—they’re *sticks of sugar.* They taste like sticks of sugar too. Probably because the ingredients consist of sugar. So what kid wouldn’t appreciate a stick of molded white sugar and corn syrup [sugar] in their candy bag after they’ve gone trick-or-treating? I know I would. And what overworked and underpaid Beacon Columnist wouldn’t appreciate a stick of molded white sugar and corn syrup [sugar] in their system while they’re writing their article? I know I would. By the way, *Popeye Candy Sticks* are absolutely disgusting. They taste like sugar, but mixed with some kind of ground up extra-strength chalk product. They’re harder to bite into than a Nokia 3310. They taste like one of science’s greatest mysteries. But they look really cool; I can’t deny that. Only a deeply underground niche could enjoy these as a pleasantry, though.  

  
Whatever candy you choose to issue out or indulge in this Halloween, have fun with it! More importantly, if you’re choosing to indulge, enjoy it. It is Halloween, after all. ’Tis the season, am I right? Please do not buy Popeye Candy Sticks, though; that is my only stipulation.
