**What makes a show truly unforgettable?** In a world overflowing with content, only a few media manage to captivate audiences and stand the test of time. Whether it’s brilliant storytelling or unforgettable characters, some shows simply outshine the rest.In this article, I’m going to be ranking my top 3 picks to watch this summer. Disclaimer: This list is definitely influenced by nostalgia, but I’ve tried to keep things as fair as possible. These picks are fun, colorful, dramatic, and, most importantly, perfect for summer. So sit back, relax, and read!  

**3. Winx Club - 7/10**
Anyone who knows me is probably a little bit surprised by the fact that I’ve ranked this at the bottom of my list, considering just how much I love it, but no amount of nostalgia can make up for the absolute dive in quality it took after season 4, the toxic relationships (I’m looking at you, Musa and Riven), and the disgustingly awful live-action reboot.  

  
  However, despite all of its flaws of now and years past, I can still acknowledge how magical the first seasons were. 
It was full of this rare, balanced use of magic and technology in rich worlds, spanning multiple dimensions. There are fairies, there are witches, there's friendship, and there's fashion. So, despite its flaws, it’s vibrant and so fun to watch if you’re into the fantasy genre and willing to stop at season 4. You have been warned!  

**2. H2O: Just Add Water - 8/10**  
 
Talk about summer! What’s more stereotypically beachy than a show about mermaids? I’ll answer it for you… nothing! 
*H2O* is a show about 3 girls and their underwater adventures after they transform into mermaids and are granted unique water-related powers. Just like the Winx Club, they learn to navigate their own personal lives, romantic relationships (still toxic), and family dynamics.  

 It’s got strong displays of early 2000s fashion, female friendships, and beautiful Australian Gold Coast setting, but… the show does tend to repeat plotlines in terms of a secret almost getting out, girls panicking, powers causing chaos, problem solved, rinse and repeat. It can get very predictable, but it’s comforting, goes perfectly with the summer theme, and, overall, a pretty decent show.  

**1. The Parent Trap - 9/10**
The only movie on this list of movies and shows…it’s *The Parent Trap* *Cue game show applause*. You and I both know this movie, but for the sake of the article, I’m going to give a brief summary anyway. This is a movie about twins who are strangers with divorced parents living on opposite sides of the Atlantic. They eventually meet each other at a summer camp and decide to swap identities. Long story short, it’s a really great movie, especially for summertime, because of its camp scenes and primarily sunny weather. It drags a little in the second half, but its humor, especially when they're sabotaging their dad's gold-digging girlfriend, more than makes up for it.
