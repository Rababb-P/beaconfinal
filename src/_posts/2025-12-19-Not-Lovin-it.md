**As I enter the chalet of mediocre cuisine, McDonalds, I can literally feel my arteries cease movement.** The aroma of fried grease and frozen patties is only matched by the oh-so-comfortable plastic furnishings. The sounds of screaming, laughing children fill the air, shortening my temper to near breaking point. 

  

 To complete my first impressions of this notorious establishment, there is a seemingly never ending line, waiting for its daily dose of cardboard filler. When at last my order can be taken, I am given the service one would expect of the coat check at the opera. 'Get your stuff and leave,' is promptly stamped on her face. It is rare to receive quality service from someone getting minimum wage, and I reluctantly accept that.
 As I sit down, ready to consume what could possibly be my last meal, I contemplate how this establishment could have become the global enterprise it is today.  

   

 I glare at the chicken nuggets, which, having the appearance of partially chewed calamari, cause me to question their animal of origin. Then at last, I attempt the consumption of the McDonald's hamburger. As the grease and what appears to be mayonnaise drip onto my hand, and the Lord's Prayer slips from my mouth, I take a bite. Delicious! I promptly consume it and then crave more, hating myself more and more for the sin I am committing. In short, the food is greatly enjoyable but with an environment similar to that of a Disneyland hell, I am reluctant to go again, preferring Wendy's for a fast food fix.
