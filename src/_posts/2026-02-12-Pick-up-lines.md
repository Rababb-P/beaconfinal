**English**
Charles Dickens might have given you Great Expectations, but I can exceed them.
It’s no wonder Big Brother’s watching you - on a scale of 1 to 10, you’re 1984.
I Sense you have a lot of Sensibility. Was that too Austen-tatious of me to point out?  

  
**Math**
you’re good at Algebra. Can you replace my X without asking Y?  

My love for you is like π... never ending.  

Can you help me with a math problem? I need to solve for x, where x = your phone number.  

  
**Biology**
I’m no organ donor but I’d be happy to give you my heart.  
  
You must be a red blood cell because you take the oxygen away from my lungs straight to my heart.  
  
If I were a nitrogenous base, I would be Adenine so I could be paired with U.  

   
**Chemistry**
Is there a science class nearby, or am I just sensing the chemistry between me and you?  
  
Do you like Chemistry? Because I’ve got my ion you!  
  
You’re hotter than a bunsen burner set to full power.  

  
**Physics**
Even if there were no gravity on Earth, I’d still fall for you.  
  
I’m attracted to you like the Earth is attracted to the Sun - with a large force inversely proportional to the distance squared.  

  
**Gym**
You must be tired from running on my mind all day.  
  
Can I get your jersey? Your name and number?  
  
Me without you is like a sneaker without laces.  

  
**Music**
I C Major potential in getting us together.  

  

**Geography**
The climate in my heart has been warmer and slightly more humid ever since I met you.  
  
There are five oceans, seven continents, and only one person like you!  

  
**History**
I’m learning about important dates in history. Wanna be one of them?  

  
**Psychology**
Are you Broca’s Aphasia? Because you leave me speechless.  
  
You light up my world like my primary visual cortex.  
  
Roses are red, violets are blue. If you were a null hypothesis, I would fail to reject you.  

  
**Computer Science**
Are you a keyboard? Because you’re my type!  
  
You’d create a leak if you were a dynamically allocated variable in a C++ program, because I’d never delete you from my life.  
  
You are my loop condition - I keep coming back to you.
