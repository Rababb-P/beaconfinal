**Sometimes, procrastinating is ok.** If you have an assignment due in, like, four weeks and you have a bajillion other things to do (namely watch the new season of your favourite show or something), then obviously procrastinate your assignment. But if it’s the night before and you’re still doomscrolling on reels, maybe put your phone down and pick up your laptop?  

  

Most teachers say the easiest way to thrive in school is to do their homework and review their class material, which is obviously an impossible task to do *every night,* but they might be onto something. 
As a former procrastinator (who relapses from time to time), my best tips are to remove any distractions. It really is that damn phone. Throw any distractions in another room, because I know you’re too lazy to go back to the room to get them when you’re bored.  

  

Another great tip is to create a schedule. Doing bio, chem, physics and math every night sounds, quite frankly, awful, but doing 30 minutes of one per night will help keep you on track, reduce your studying time for tests, and leave you a free night to binge that new season. 
I don’t really know how to end this, since I procrastinated with this article and it’s due right now, but, with these new tips, you will not be in my position anymore.
