**It’s actually a Royal Christmas Theory—because for some reason, every Hallmark movie seems to exist in the same universe.** And no, I’m not talking about the usual traits of universal whiteness, constant lying, or everyone mysteriously being both American (specifically from New York) and British at the same time. I mean the deeper connection between the films themselves: the world where *A Christmas Prince, The Princess Switch, The Royal Treatment, and even The Princess Diaries* are all secretly intertwined.  

  

We begin with *A Christmas Prince,* where Amber, a journalist, lies about her identity to pose as the tutor for the soon-to-be king’s little sister. Her motive is to write an article (twin!) about the secrets of the royal family and the prince. In the process, she discovers that the latter is adopted, almost costing him the throne. But all is resolved when she finds a letter from the past king amending the laws that prevent adopted children from inheriting the throne and writes an article about how good of a guy he is. Oh, and of course they fall in love and get engaged.  

  
  
In *The Princess Switch,* the lie involves Margaret, a princess, and Stacy, a New York baker, who swap identities for a few days. The princess inevitably falls for the baker’s best friend, the baker falls for the prince, and the entire nation of Belgravia just… rolls with it.  

  
  
In *The Royal Treatment*, we get another New Yorker—this time a hairdresser named Izzy with a horrible fake accent—who gets a suspicious royal phone call to cut the hair of the prince of Lavinia. You guessed it! They also fall in love.  

  
  
Finally, *The Princess Diaries.* It's the classic movie/book about a girl named Mia who finds out she’s the heir to the kingdom of Genovia and is forced to balance princess duties with normal high school. In the end, she manages to find balance, discovers who she truly is, and finds her happy ending.  

  

 At this point you’re probably thinking the plot summaries are great and all, but how are they connected? What’s the theory? Stay with me here.  

   

Every movie takes place on a planet I call Christmastopia. Each continent represents a Christmas Hallmark movie genre, such as City Christmas, Vineyard Christmas, Bakery Christmas, and of course, Royal Christmas. On the Royal continent, there are five kingdoms: Aldovia and Penglia, Belgravia, Lavinia, and Genovia. *cue suspenseful music* They are all under a curse placed by evil Santa—the forgotten twin of Santa Claus. He, once a boy blinded by jealousy for his brother, would eventually be banished by his family for believing that Christmas should be about work and seriousness. Consumed by evil, he cursed all the lands to have single, sad princes who are allergic to responsibility.  

  

This is why everything is the same in these universes. The generic princes, elaborate lies, predominantly white casts, and supposed New Yorkers with terrible accents aren’t just patterns, they’re evidence of this theory!  

  

Additionally, note the following proof: Aldovia has a map where Penglia and Belgravia are mentioned, and its king and queen attend the coronation of the princess from *A Princess Switch*. Plus, the hairdresser from *The Royal Treatment* references the fact that Lavinia is a country next to Aldovia, and jokingly dismisses the call for the royal haircut by saying, "Yeah, and I'm the Queen of Genovia."  

  

Clearly, they’re all connected by my theory, and I guess maybe also the dialogue and hints from each movie. But it’s mostly my theory, where they all live in Christmastopia, cursed by Evil Santa, and destined to repeat the same royal romantic Christmas patterns forever.
