**How soon is too soon?** “Love bombing” is a term associated with the rapid and manipulative acceleration of a romantic relationship, usually without any logic or reason. Commonly starting heated and unrealistic relationships that end abusively within a short period of time, love bombing is normalized and often romanticised in the media. In the movies *Tangled* and *How To Lose A Guy In 10 Days,* it is shown as something cutesy and fun. In this article, I will explain the psychological reasoning behind love bombing and its unrealistic representation in the media.	Firstly, why do people love-bomb? According to *Psychology Today,* love bombing could stem from narcissism, desire to control or manipulate, familial normality in overall affection, or codependency. The need to manipulate originates from an intrusive want for power. This could occur from close proximity to toxic relationships with unfair power dynamics. In many cultures, marriage is considered necessary within a certain timeframe. Ordinarily, this timeframe is short. Being exposed to these customs could suggest that any future relationships need to be rushed and perfected immediately. The ability to function and have a life without one’s partner is incredibly important for a healthy relationship. All of these factors create a tendency to love-bomb in a relationship.  

  
Secondly, love bombing is hidden and made positive in several Disney films. Though ending happily, *Tangled* is no exception to an unrealistic relationship made through love bombing. Rapunzel and Eugene meet and over a time span of two whole days, fall in love wholeheartedly. If this story was told presently, the person would  be judged for their recklessness and lack of realism. Blindsided by Disney magic, *Tangled* is another illusion of “love at first sight.”
In addition, a movie called *How To Lose A Guy In 10 Days* reinvented how quickly a relationship can turn from bliss to horror. Writers Andie and Ben are instructed to perform experiments to become enchanted with each other, then crash and burn in less than two weeks. The advancement of their relationship is intense and sprouting with emotion. This makes the downfall even more painful. With an absurd amount of time to process, relationships like these can cause immense hurt for either side. *How To Lose A Guy In 10 Days* is realistic if the romantic deposition is ignored.  

  
In short, take time to decide and fall for one another. If love bombing is suspected, have a conversation with your  partner to decide on next steps. Love shouldn’t be rushed.
