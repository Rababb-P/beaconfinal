**Dear Readers,**  

  
**On May 21st, 2025, I had the pleasure of writing a 1000-word essay in one school night.** Unfortunately for me, I had not read the book I was supposed to write this essay on. This experience taught me many valuable lessons, and my success through this journey is something I want to share with all of you. Here is my process:  

  

**Step 1: Find a Summary of My Book**  

  
Sadly, a summary of my book was not available on SparkNotes. This led me to feel lots of joy and definitely not despair. Luckily, I was able to find a summary of my book from a website that required me to accept some questionable terms and conditions.  

  
  **Step 2: Start Writing**  

    
This is often the hardest step in writing an essay. It was definitely the hardest step for me. I chose to write the first two lines of my essay, then move on to Step 3.  

  

 
**Step 3: Procrastinate**  

  
Writing those two lines took a lot of energy and discipline. To recharge, I began to watch Instagram Reels. A good rule of thumb for this is watching around 30 minutes of Reels per two essay lines written.  

  
**Step 4: 3 AM**  

  
At 3 AM, I realized the Instagram Reel rule wasn’t going to let me finish in time. So, for the next hour, I put Subway Surfers gameplay videos on half of my monitor and wrote whatever I could think of for my essay on the other half. The Subway Surfers gameplay was essential to keep my focus during this last-minute grind.  

  
  **Step 5: Sleep**  

    
This step is fairly straightforward. I had some trouble sleeping that night, most likely due to the dread of realizing my teacher would have to read my awful essay. My solution was to watch more Instagram Reels. 
I would rate this experience a 0/10 due to the stress I felt that night and the sleep deprivation I felt the next morning. Unfortunately, this will probably end up happening again in the near future, as procrastination is one of my favourite hobbies. I hope you never have to use this plan, as it will probably not give you the result you are hoping for; however, feel free to do whatever your heart desires. At the end of the day, all that matters are the Reels we watched along the way.
