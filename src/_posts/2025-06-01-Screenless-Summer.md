**You’ve probably decided what you’re doing to do this summer - maybe you’ll do some volunteering, complete a co-op program, or get a job.** Maybe you’re going on vacation and exploring new parts of the world. Or maybe you’re just going to enjoy and have fun as much as you can during those two elusive summer months. But let’s face it. Whether it’s endlessly scrolling short-form content on TikTok or playing mindless, weirdly addictive mobile games that are probably intended for a much younger audience, you’re probably going to stare at screens a lot this summer. When September rolls around in no time, you’ll be left wondering where all your time went. That’s why I’m going to provide you with some tips for how you can pull off of a bit of a digital detox this summer vacation.  
  
First off, let’s start with an easy tip - don’t rush things! When you’re trying to change your lifestyle in a major way, making such a drastic change like completely shutting yourself off from technology is not really a great way to do it.  

  The withdrawal symptoms and the urges to go on your phone will just be severely worse, and you’ll probably end up giving up on the whole thing. Instead, set small goals and try to reduce your technology use gradually over time. For example, maybe just start with screen time limits that slowly get lower - or delete certain particularly addicting apps off of your phone while keeping others. Understand that the process is going to be slow and grueling: you’re not going to magically lose your phone addiction overnight.  

   
One important revelation that is really going to help you pull this off is realizing that your brain doesn’t constantly *need* stimulation. Humans aren’t built for that. You don’t *need* to be watching a *YouTube* video or TV while you’re eating a meal: you can just focus on what you’re eating, focus on the taste, the smell, the texture. You don’t need to listen to music or scroll on TikTok while you’re on a bus. Just try to be present in the moment you’re living and discover the natural stimulus within your environment. There are plenty of interesting things going on all around you, you just won’t realize it while you’re on your phone!  

  And finally, a tip that’s really simple but very effective in limiting your screen time: simply be curious and ask tons of questions! Be knowledge hungry and try to learn new things every chance you get. We’re so curious and inquisitive when we’re younger, we all have the capability to be that way, so why wouldn’t you be? Noticing the world around you and being curious about it is such a great way to cut down on the time you spend on your phone.  
   
You may have noticed that I never wrote about *why* you should try to use screens less: deep down, you already know why. Everyone knows they should be on their phone less, but rarely does anyone actually make any change in their day-to-day life. You have nothing to lose and everything to gain, so just try it. And remember, no one is stopping you from having the most fulfilling, memorable summer but yourself.
