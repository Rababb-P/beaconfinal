**Our digital world has created infinite possibilities for self-expression** such as Instagram profiles, Pinterest boards, and even Goodreads or Letterboxd reviews – but the playlist might be the best of them all! Here are three reasons why you should make more of them.  
  
  
1.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The possibilities are endless  
Music has never been more accessible. At the touch of a finger, you can explore local artists, diverse genres, or travel through time periods. The best part – you can amalgamate whichever songs you wish to, for whatever purposes your heart desires! Tailor a backing track for your next workout or choose 10 songs that throw you back to summer. If you’re feeling uninspired, user or AI-made playlists offer an endless selection – everything from your favourite book character to the oddly specific life event you just experienced. But making your own is an art form – a unique way to express your feelings and capture a momentary version of yourself.  

  

2.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Connect with friends  
Making, enjoying, and interacting with music has been a major part of many cultures, and for good reason! According to
Dr. Cathy Malchiodi, the happiness humans feel when experiencing art comes from ourevolutionary instincts. Through apps such as Spotify, you can create shared playlists, or even listen to the same music in real time with your friends! Shared playlists are perfect for events, or just a mutual appreciation for the same music. If you’re feeling creative, create a playlist for someone else!  

  

3.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Music is good for you  
According to John Hopkins Medicine, “There are few things that stimulate the brain the way music does.” Music listening is proven to reduce anxiety, blood pressure, and pain, as well as improve sleep quality, mood, mental alertness, and memory. New music stimulates the brain in different ways relative to familiar music, which is essential for healthy aging. All the more reason to listen to and make more playlists!
