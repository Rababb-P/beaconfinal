**Gibbons - “It’s a gibbon, I’m bananas for you!”**
If you don’t know what a gibbon is, don’t worry! I didn’t know either before writing this. Gibbons are actually very small tailless apes native to the tropical forests of Southeast Asia. They're known to sing together with their chosen life partners to reinforce their bond. Apart from that, gibbons showcase their physical affection through mutual grooming, and in some cases, holding hands. I highly recommend finding a video of gibbons singing. It’s incredibly ghostly yet beautiful.

**Geese - “My love for you is simply humon-goose”**
Ahh, geese, our winged friends at MCI, and the only animals on this list native to Canada. They poop… a lot. They honk… a lot. They chase people… a lot. We all know this, but we should also recognize how faithful they are to their partners. When a goose is injured, their mate will protect them fiercely, often refusing to abandon them, even if in danger themselves.
