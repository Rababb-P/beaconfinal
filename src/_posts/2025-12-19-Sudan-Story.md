**Imagine a life where you can’t buy your favourite Tim Horton’s order or new Apple AirPods; not because you forgot your wallet, but because having enough food, clean water, and safety is already a daily struggle.** Many of us take small comforts for granted while kids across the world yearn to have even one healthy meal a day. Millions of children aren’t just missing out on privileges - they don’t even have the fundamental rights they deserve. This is the life of the thousands of children famished and abused in Sudan.  

  
Sudan has endured years of genocide, each year deepening the crisis and making survival harder. Since April 2023, the Sudanese Civil War has stolen not only lives, but futures. Today, “14 million children are out of school” (UNICEF), trapped in a war they are not responsible for.  

  
The crisis in Sudan is incredibly violent. Children are being assaulted and torn away from their families, while civilians witness brutality inflicted upon their own relatives and neighbours. According to IPC’s food security report, “more than 21 million people across Sudan are facing high levels of acute food insecurity.” (Elfatih). To put this into perspective, it would take 21, 000 Martingrove-sized schools just to hold the number of people facing hunger. 

  
After reading this, there is no doubt you will return to your daily life, but a little compassion can go a long way. These civilians are facing this crisis because of circumstances far beyond their control. With the resources you have at your fingertips, there are meaningful ways to support those in Sudan. 
The first step you can take is getting involved on social media and becoming more aware of the stories coming out of Sudan. Sharing educational posts and news updates helps more people understand the crisis.

By following Sudanese journalists, watching explainer videos, and reading NGO reports, you can stay informed about the ongoing tragedy. 
Supporting reputable organizations is another powerful way to help the Sudanese children. If you cannot donate yourself, try sharing donation links to people who might be able to contribute. Participating in online fundraisers also makes a real difference.  

  
You can also take part in digital awareness campaigns. Create Google Slides, posters, or infographics to educate peers and get the people around you involved. Even small consistent actions can help bring attention to a crisis that desperately needs it.
Helping Sudan is a choice. Ultimately, it’s up to you to decide how you want to make a difference. Widen your perspective, stand with those who are suffering, and use your voice to bring Sudan closer to freedom.
