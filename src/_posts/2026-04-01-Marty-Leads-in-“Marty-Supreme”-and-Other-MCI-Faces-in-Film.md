Imagine a world where, instead of actresses like Emma Stone or Michael B. Jordan, movies and TV shows cast staff from Martingrove. Instead of seeing Timothée Chalamet play a ping-pong enthusiast in the world-famous Marty Supreme, imagine a staff member taking his place. Rather than choosing a teacher we all know, there might be an even more obvious contender for Marty Supreme: Marty the Bear himself. MCI’s mascot, already seen in many BAC sports reels, could easily step into the role of the ping-pong champion Marty Mauser strives to be. I asked teachers and students which members of the Martingrove community would be perfect fits for characters in the movies and TV shows we watch today.



The first teacher I interviewed was Mr. Dunne. His joyful and enthusiastic personality resembles many characters from TV and movies.  Some students said he reminded them of Ms. Frizzle, sharing traits like encouragement and a fun presence that make students want to learn more. When asked which character he relates to most, he said he feels like Peter B. Parker from *Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse:* “I like to think that I'm like the older, out-of-shape Spider-Man. The parallels are there: his past is filled with failure, his love interest is more successful than him, he's forced into a mentorship role he's ill prepared for, and his Uncle Ben was killed by a burglar he could have stopped.”

  

Mr. Nav saw himself as the Flash, imagining what it would be like to have the hero’s incredible speed and control over time, but some students at Martingrove had different characters in mind when describing him. One said he gave off the vibes of SpongeBob SquarePants because of his cheerful attitude and friendliness.  Another comparison was Yoon Sang-hwa from *Train to Busan,* a strong and fearless character remembered for his kindness and willingness to protect others. Other characters mentioned include the Genie from *Aladdin* and Mario from *Super Mario Bros.*

  

Finally, speaking to Ms. Wisam, she couldn’t think of any characters she resembled, but we got some answers from students! One student said she had the same enthusiasm and encouragement as Joy from *Inside Out.* Another student said she reminded her of Elle Woods from *Legally Blonde,* who is pretty, fashionable and intelligent.

  

Although Martingrove staff may not appear on the big screen, their personalities remind students of the memorable characters they watch every day. With TIFF’s *Next Wave Film Festival* around the corner, let’s hope to find our very own MCI star, like Marty, on the big screen.
