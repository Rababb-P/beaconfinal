Artificial intelligence is not a sleeping beast. It is awake, and we will only look back and see that when it is too late. According to an article from Western University, , the global usage of water by AI will be the same as that of half of the U.K by 2027. By 2028, AI will use up as much electricity annually as 22% of U.S. households. Furthermore, much of the energy needed to power AI does not come from clean sources. The world is already struggling to mitigate global warming and climate change as it is; A.I. is just stirring the pot even more.  

  
Additionally, people have begun to depend heavily on AI for studying, generating content for social media, entertainment, work, and even simply writing emails.  Researchers at MIT have conducted studies showing that using AI makes the brain lazier over time and weakens the user’s ability to form original thoughts, as well as negatively impacting their long-term memory and learning skills.
All this is not to say that AI does not have any benefits. Sometimes, when a student is finding it difficult to learn or understand something, it can be useful to get the gears turning if you are stuck.
In my opinion, the threat of AI can be compared to that of plastic. Plastic is like a monster that has you surrounded and is impossible to defeat. That does not mean that plastic doesn’t come with its advantages. For example, plastic products make it easier and more affordable to administer certain medicines. However, because plastic was allowed to grow unimpeded without the  control of strong laws early on, it has become impossible to control.  

  
It is essential to preserve the flame, but prevent it from growing into a fire. If the world can come together and do what it couldn’t when plastic was born, we can both reap the benefits of AI, and keep it on a tight leash so it doesn’t come back to bite us.
