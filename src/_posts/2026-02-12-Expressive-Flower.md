**With Valentine’s Day approaching, roses are bound to be a topic of interest.** Roses have been associated with love for a long time, and they come in many different colours, each holding their own meaning. This makes roses a great way to express one’s feelings, because the color of the rose can signify so much, even when no words are said. They are comparable to emojis, before they were invented, and could be the ancestor of the heart emoji. Some of the most common rose colors that can be seen are red, pink, and yellow, so let’s explore each one.  

  

Red roses symbolize romantic love. Their bold color helps convey strong feelings, and are more tangible and memorable than a red heart sent over a text message. They are very common on Valentine’s Day, and many people give their romantic partner a red rose on this day to signify their love. 
Pink roses are also very common on Valentine’s Day. They can signify many things including gratitude, admiration, and happiness. Someone might give a pink rose to acknowledge somebody that they appreciate or to thank them.  

  
A more uncommon rose color that is not seen as much on Valentine’s Day is the yellow rose. In  Victorian times, it was associated with jealousy; however, today yellow roses symbolize friendship and happiness. A yellow rose is a nice gift to show your appreciation for a good friend.
On Valentine’s Day, many people celebrate love by giving roses to those they care about. It can be confusing to know what the different colours can convey; however, knowing that detail can make the gift all the more special. Unlike a 2-dimensional heart emoji that is sent and forgotten, a rose can be treasured much more dearly.
