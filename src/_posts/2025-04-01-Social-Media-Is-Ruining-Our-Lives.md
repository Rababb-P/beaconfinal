The social media algorithm. The magic system that knows your age, your likes, and your dislikes as soon as you click on an app. In short, it’s a complex set of rules and calculations used by the media to determine which kind of content you’re most likely to click on. They record your history, dislikes, and likes. This system "programs" you to keep using the platform, giving them more engagement, and more money.

Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikToks - all of them. Humans have now developed the need for various types of serotonin because we’ve become accustomed to the rate of simulation.
