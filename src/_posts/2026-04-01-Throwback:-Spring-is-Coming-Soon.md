**We all love winter, don’t we?** The bone-chilling temperatures, the sun setting at literally 5pm, shovelling snow, slipping on ice. Surely these are all crucial to Canadian winters. Winter often feels slow and boring, but don’t worry because spring and summer will be here before you even realize it. In fact, this somewhat chill period of time might actually be great for planning what to do when spring and summer do finally arrive.Here are my suggestions of fun and not-so-fun things you should add to your list.

  

1. Let’s start off simple. You should definitely declutter and get organized. Spring cleaning is great!!!! Organize your notes, bedroom, or wardrobe. Essentially anything that can be organized should be organized. Here’s a pro tip: Get rid of anything that doesn’t bring value to your life.

  

2. Find some great books to read. It really is that simple. Choose a genre that you think you might actually have some interest in, and start reading. I’ve heard Frankenstein is amazing, but remember, picture books are great too, and they often have the best messages.

  

3. Choose a sport, any sport, and just play.  It could be anything from chess to wrestling- whatever you find enjoyable that brings you happiness.

  

4. Go to the beach. This is always fun. No explanation required.

  

5. Maybe get a summer job. It’s a great way to earn some quick money while simultaneously developing interpersonal relationships. There really is no downside.

  

6. Take time to breathe, love yourself, and do what you really enjoy. It is easy to forget to do this while living in this busy world, but it really is important.
