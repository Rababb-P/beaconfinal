**For MCI’s 60th reunion, I had the pleasure and privilege of interviewing one of our own alumni.** Ms. Goodwillie, an entrepreneur who has overcome the journey of self-discovery through her lengthy career and education path is an inspiration to students today. I was particularly interested in her unique creativity, designing purses and bags for a number of people.

  

During her time at MCI, entrepreneurship in the form of handbags wasn’t exactly “on her radar.” Like many students now, her main goal was to attend a university in business management so her high school schedule mainly consisted of math courses. However, going on this path is something she does not regret. She explains, “Because of the degree I got I was able to get a job directly out of university... eventually helped me to get a [later] job working at a friend's tech company which allowed me more freedom to then pursue entrepreneurship.”

  

Ms. Goodwillie had an admirable business spark in her, a spark that couldn’t be ignored. Unfortunately, creating a business isn’t all charms and zippers. When she first quit her stable office job, her parents weren’t supportive. From their perspective, their daughter quitting a job that keeps her financially healthy isn’t a good idea. They eventually came around but having the support of one’s parents is extremely important. She reasons, “It would have been nicer if they were supportive right from the beginning.”

  

Furthermore, her favourite part of her business is being able to let her mind wander when designing. In a business where trends are changing constantly, she is always thinking of “new ways to make money in my business.” AI is now a newly included part of her management. She uses it to visualize a bag’s design for a customer or even to find new ways to make money. Ms. Goodwillie has proved that AI can be a useful tool in the business world.

  

From a young age, Ms. Goodwillie always knew she wanted to work for herself.  She started working as a personal trainer and did clothing alterations on the side. With time, her liking for sewing grew and she decided on making bags because “they didn't need to fit like clothing...would be easier.” Ms. Goodwillie started her entrepreneurship on Etsy, a site all of us could potentially start our future businesses on. Without any formal sewing knowledge, she taught herself how to sew by remaking people’s bags. Her dedication and hard work are why she is running a successful business today.

  

In conclusion, her message for current MCI students or anyone who needs it is that if you are unsure of your future or what you want to do with your life, that’s okay. Soon enough, you could be making a purse for Kristen Bell to hold in a TV show or several bags for NBA players. She says, “It took me until I was 31 years old to be working for myself, so it took a while. But this has been the best decision that I made and I don't regret any of the decisions I made that got me to where I am now.”
