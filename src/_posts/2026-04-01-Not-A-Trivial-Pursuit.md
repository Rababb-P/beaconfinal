**When you think of Martingrove’s Trivia Team, you probably imagine a bunch of nerds sitting around in a dark, stuffy room, arguing over whether the ‘real’ capital of South Africa is Cape Town, Pretoria, or Johannesburg.** As the team captain, I can assure you that is not true! The room we practice in (107) is very large and well-lit. However, to dispel some of these more pervasive myths about our team, let me update you on a few of the amazing accomplishments and extraordinary events that have occurred since the start of the year!

  

Martingrove’s Trivia Intramurals concluded with an Awards Ceremony on January 19th! The teams were sorted into Junior and Senior brackets and played a full round robin of games against all other teams in their group. The top 4 teams in each bracket went to the semifinals to determine which 2 teams would face off for the championship! It was an exciting tournament and all teams displayed trivia knowledge, teamwork, and sportsmanship.

  

Congratulations to the first-place Junior team, Garlic Bread (Graham P., Vivaan S., Nathan C.) and the first-place Senior team, Pulchritudinous Plankton Princesses (Zoe S.-J., Yang Xin Z., Kaia R.)! They have been immortalized on a new trophy, displayed in room 107 for all to see.

  

Additionally, on Saturday, February 21st, Martingrove’s halls filled with trivia players from across the Toronto West Region, each hoping to secure a spot at the Consensus Trivia National Tournament in May. MCI hosted 9 different schools to play a full day of trivia games, facing tough competition as our trivia team battled against their opponents.

  

Our A-Team went undefeated all day, winning all 10 of their games against Toronto’s toughest. This means they placed first in the region and automatically qualified for the National Tournament!  As well, our B-Team placed second in their division by winning 7 games, an amazing accomplishment! The Bears had awesome team play, intelligence, and sportsmanship throughout the tournament, working together and showing off their trivia skills.
