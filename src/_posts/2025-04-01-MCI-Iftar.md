**MCI’s annual Iftar, organized by the Muslim Student Association (MSA), is a cherished tradition that brings together students, teachers, and community members to celebrate the spirit of Ramadan.** This highly anticipated event fosters a sense of unity, inclusivity, and cultural appreciation, making it one of the most significant gatherings at the school. As the sun sets and the call to prayer signals the breaking of the fast, attendees share a meal that not only nourishes the body but also strengthens the bonds within the diverse school community. The event is open to all students, regardless of religious background, emphasizing the values of togetherness, compassion, and understanding.  

  
Planning for the annual Iftar begins weeks in advance, with MSA members dedicating their time and effort to ensure everything runs smoothly. Decorations often include traditional Ramadan lanterns, crescent moons, and elegant table settings that reflect the significance of the holy month. A key highlight of the event is the carefully curated menu, featuring a variety of dishes that cater to different cultural tastes, including dates, samosas, rice dishes with chicken or falafel with potatoes and salad.  

Beyond the meal, the event includes a program filled with meaningful activities and discussions. Guest speakers, often local religious leaders or community figures, are invited to share insights about Ramadan, the importance of fasting, and the broader values of patience, gratitude, and charity. There are also student-led reflections where individuals share Quran recitation. These speeches allow both Muslim and non-Muslim attendees to gain a deeper understanding of Islamic traditions and the spiritual aspects of the holy month.  

  
One of the most impactful moments of the Iftar is when students pray together, reinforcing the communal aspect of the event. A designated prayer space is arranged within the school, allowing Muslim students to perform the Maghrib prayer after breaking their fast. This moment of unity, where individuals from different backgrounds come together in a shared spiritual experience, embodies the essence of Ramadan and reinforces the importance of faith and community.  

  

Ultimately, the Martingrove Iftar is more than just a meal—it is a celebration of diversity, a testament to the school’s commitment to inclusivity, and a moment for students to come together in an environment of mutual respect and learning. It fosters a sense of belonging for Muslim students while also educating others about the values and traditions of Ramadan. Each year, the event continues to grow, reinforcing the importance of cultural appreciation and the power of unity within the school community..
