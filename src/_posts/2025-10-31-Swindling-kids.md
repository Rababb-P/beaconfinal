Every year on Halloween, many people go trick-or-treating. It is the one day when kids have the freedom to enjoy every candy and treat they want. As your bag of treats gets fuller with every house visited, imagine the horror when the treat you get is not as sweet as you would expect.  



I still remember one time when I went trick-or-treating with my sister, and we knocked on the door of a house expecting a tasty treat, just to receive hand sanitizer! It was definitely an interesting experience. Although it was funny at the time, this is a horrible thing to hand out on Halloween. When people are trick-or-treating, they want chocolates, or candy, or something tasty. Handing out hand sanitizer can be a bit of a disappointment, ruining the mood.   

  
Another common thing some people might hand out on Halloween are fruits and vegetables. Halloween is the one day when kids can get as much candy as they want without any restrictions or healthy food rules. Handing out vegetables or fruits would completely ruin that night of freedom. It would also be terribly upsetting to receive a vegetable or fruit instead of a delicious treat that kids may have been expecting as a reward for all the time they put in going house to house.  

  
Furthermore, some people might decide to hand out their own homemade sweet treats on Halloween. Maybe they baked cookies or something sweet and decided to hand it out. Although this is a generous thought, it might not be enjoyed by everyone, as some people feel uncomfortable eating something unpackaged from someone they do not know, or they may have food allergies, in which case they might not be able to eat the treat. That is why it is always better to stick to packaged treats.  

  
In conclusion, Halloween is the only day of the year when there are no restrictions or rules about healthy eating. It is a day that everyone should be able to enjoy and have fun. By handing out things on Halloween that everyone does not enjoy, such as hand sanitizer, fruits or vegetables, and homemade goods, it can take the joy out of Halloween. Sometimes it is just better to stick to the tried and true, by handing out chocolates and candies, and ensure a fun time for everyone.
