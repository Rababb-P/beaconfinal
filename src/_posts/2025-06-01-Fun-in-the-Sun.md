**Are you tired of burning every summer?** I know I sure am. As a very white man, I burn just about every year, so allow me to guide you on how to tan rather than burn this summer.
Firstly, a stray hot day with UV 8 in the middle of May might make you want to get out there to tan right away, but this leads to you burning easily. Trust me, I know. Instead, start at a low UV level to build a base tan before heading out in the higher UV levels.  
Another important way to avoid sunburn and maximize your tanning potential is to start with an SPF sunscreen to protect your untanned skin. Once your tan has begun to develop, move on to a tanning oil that has SPF. Finally, you can use a tanning oil, but be careful, as tanning oils amplify how much UV penetrates your skin, so use with caution.  

  

  The next step to mastering the art of tanning is to be conscious of your tan lines. Forgetting to take off your watch before tanning can cause you to have a tan line on your wrist for the rest of the summer - that's best to avoid. Tan consistently, especially if you are heading out in the sun specifically to tan. Make sure that you keep track of how long you're spending on your front and back to keep your tan as even as possible.  

     
My final tip to you is also coincidentally the last step any time you tan, which is to moisturize. This is the most important step to make you feel good and to help your tan last. Moisturizing will stop your skin from drying and peeling, which keeps your fantastic tan on longer.
Remember, as someone who burns every year without fail, I am the leading authority on tanning. Don’t forget these tips to achieve your perfect tan!
