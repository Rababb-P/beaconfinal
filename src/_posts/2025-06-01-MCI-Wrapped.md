**Somehow, 2025 is halfway over – and a school year we thought would last forever is now coming to a close.** Between the endless procrastination, and a January that felt like it lasted a year on its own, there is much to look back on and appreciate.  

How quickly time passes by. During this school year, there have been many events that ended in laughter. At the beginning, everyone was new and daunting, and that sense of unfamiliarity took over and created a pit in my stomach. However, as more and more time elapsed, that sense started disappearing, and with it, some truly wonderful people started appearing. Some of the very first school events, including the Grade 8 Open House, Homecoming, and Nuit Blanche, fostered a sense of community at MCI. Homecoming was SAC’s earliest event and it was jam-packed with food, dogs, and off-key karaoke.  



 It was truly a memorable first event, even if everyone left halfway through *Top Gun.* Another incredible Martingrove moment was definitely the Winter Talent Show. Funny club videos, showstopping vocals, and humorous skits made everyone laugh. Plus, who could forget about Mr. Farrow’s legendary emcee skills?  

One of the obvious highlights of the year is the BAC and its amazing sports teams. All three seasons were packed with excitement, and it was incredible to see our Bears shine, especially during home games. From the exhausting final stretches in cross country, to the volleyball serve that seals the match, to the defense that blocks the opponent’s last-second hockey shot, every moment was thrilling and unforgettable.  

Of course, who can forget the extraordinary clubs that held so many different events this year?  



  The recent Asian Heritage Month assembly and the BSA’s Black Heritage Month assembly were wonderful showcases of MCI’s diversity.  

Also, TEDx’s amazing inspirational speakers were a treat to see. Martingrove Stage Company created stellar student-led performances during the NTS Dramafest, and thanks to the Music Council’s dedication, the String Orchestra and Concert Band delivered magical performances during both the Winter and Spring Music Nights. Every single club has helped shape Martingrove into what it is today, a wonderful place for everyone.  

Though it may feel like everything is ending too quickly, (or too slowly) Martingrove has done a wonderful job at keeping the students entertained throughout the year. Let’s hope we retain and improve this level of enjoyment in the future. See you next year!
