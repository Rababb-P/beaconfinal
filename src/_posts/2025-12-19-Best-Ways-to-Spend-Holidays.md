**As the winter holidays approach, it can be difficult to find fun ways to spend your time, especially since there’s no school.** However, there are still plenty of enjoyable things you can do to make the most of the holidays!  

  

Skating is an excellent activity to do over the winter break. There are plenty of places in Toronto where you can skate outdoors and enjoy the fresh winter air. Some outdoor skating places include the Bentway, Sherbourne Common, and Nathan Phillips Square. Skating is also fun to do with your family and friends.  

  

Additionally, tobogganing is another enjoyable winter pastime. It’s more fun to do when there is plenty of snow. The snowy hills are like waterslides except with snow! Tobogganing is very beginner friendly and you can also race others to add to the excitement. Some places in Toronto that you can go tobogganing at include Centennial Park, Fairmount Park, and Bickford Park.  

  

If you prefer to stay warm and cozy indoors, you should try baking. You can experiment with different recipes and make tasty treats, while simultaneously strengthening your baking skills. You can find many recipes on YouTube or baking websites such as Sally’s Baking Addiction. You can also share the treats you make with your friends and family to spread more holiday cheer.  

  

Even when the winter brings snow and freezing temperatures, your holidays do not have to be boring and miserable. You can still make the most out of the holidays and enjoy countless fun activities.
