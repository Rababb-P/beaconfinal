**Many people think of going to a library when it comes to studying because libraries have a treasure trove of books and knowledge that can assist in research and studying.** However, today, the internet has access to every book and tons of information, far surpassing what’s available in one library, and can be accessed anywhere. Yet, so many people still choose to study in libraries. So what exactly is it about libraries that make them good places to study?

  

For one, libraries are designed to be quiet places. It is an unspoken rule that when you enter a library, you must be quiet and speak softly. This makes libraries a quiet place to study without much disturbance. Many folks  are able to focus better when it is quiet because they are less likely to get distracted from their work, and can be more productive.

  

Secondly, libraries offer resources that cannot be found as easily online. There are millions of resources that appear in one internet search, which can be difficult to search through. Libraries have a more limited but very organized selection, so it is not an overwhelming amount of information all at once. Librarians also know a lot about the books and resources available at a library, and can help you find things, or recommend good books. This can make research easier. Reading physical books also helps some people study better, especially relative to reading from a screen. Most libraries also have  computers, so you can use the internet.

  

Furthermore, libraries have a great learning atmosphere, and have many resources that can make studying easier. For example, some libraries have private rooms that people can use so that they can work more peacefully, or focus on a group project. Many libraries also have different areas to study. For example, some libraries might have an outdoor area, should you need to get some fresh air, which could improve your focus.

  

Although to some people libraries today might appear obsolete with the development of the internet, they do offer a lot more than just books and information, and are a great place for everyone to visit, whether to study, work on a project, or read.
