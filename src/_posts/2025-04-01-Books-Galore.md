**On Wednesday March 5th, the LLC opened its doors for an exciting event.** The Book Swap, a longstanding Martingrove tradition, was back for the first time since the pandemic. In the preceding weeks, students and staff brought in piles of books in exchange for tickets that would buy them new reads in turn. The grand total? Over 900 books were collected!  

  

Having accidentally joined the organizing committee, I worked alongside dedicated LLC volunteers Noori Kim, Georgia Wu, and Jihyo Lee to set up for the Swap.  

  

Over several days, under Ms. Ferracane’s and Ms. Feller’s watchful eyes, we spent hours categorizing all the books and arranging them on tables. It was extremely rewarding to see our efforts culminate in a successful event. I encourage fellow students to get involved in the many volunteering opportunities the LLC has to offer. Shoutout to Ms. Ferracane and everyone who helped make this possible!  

  

The LLC provided complimentary tickets to English classes who visited in the morning to expand their literary horizons. Then, at lunchtime, the stacks were left to the mercy of MCI’s most voracious readers who could now pick up any number of books entirely for free.  

  

“It really brings together the community,” Ms. Feller explained. Her words of wisdom ring true; the Book Swap is a unique occasion to unite the Martingrove community through a universally enjoyed medium—books! Even if you might think reading isn’t your thing, I guarantee there was something for everyone: bestselling fantasy series, bone-chilling horror paperbacks, cookbooks, picture books for younger siblings - you name it.  

  

And if you missed out this year—don’t fret! The Book Swap will be back next year, hopefully on an even larger scale, and everyone is encouraged to bring in and take home even more books. If you’re lucky, there may be some stragglers left over in the library. Happy reading!
