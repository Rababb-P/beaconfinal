**Deciding on a Halloween costume is a very difficult, time-consuming process for some.** Others decide to dress as a cheetah 5 minutes before school and move on with their day. Both kinds of people are valid, and both feel the Halloween spirit nonetheless. However, with Halloween falling on a Friday, Halloweekend is upon us, which means three different costumes may be required, and not everyone is looking to curate crazy, super-niche costumes. So, to help those feeling uninspired, here are my predictions for the most popular costumes of 2025!  

  
**Superman**

With the new movie, and the ease and gender-neutrality of this costume, I foresee many different varieties of Superman. Whether it’s the full outfit, or just an open white button down with the Superman logo peaking through, this will definitely be a popular costume.  

  
**Wednesday**

After season 1 of Wednesday, black dresses and two braids were all the rage, and I don’t doubt that this year will be any different after the new season. If we’re switching it up a bit, a great duo costume could be the characters Enid and Agnes (specifically their dance outfits).  

  
**Ladybugs**

Last year, deer were all the rage. This year, I think it’s going to be ladybugs. They’re such cute and simple costumes, and can be turned into a group costume with other bugs (bumblebee, caterpillar, etc.).  

  
**Patrick Bateman**

This is a very cool and easy costume that pays homage to horror movies and the overall scary vibe of Halloween. It’s been around for a long time, but it’s still popular for a reason.  

   
**Monster High**

Such a great costume for groups of any size. There are so many characters to choose from and they’re very easy to dress up or down. I’m excited to see these costumes this year.  

  
**Elphaba and Glinda**

Returning for a second year, these costumes are bound to appear, especially with the new movie. I’m hoping to see some variations of these costumes: maybe Glinda's blue outfit instead?  

  
While I love to see the creative costumes every year, high school sometimes gets in the way, so these costumes are perfect for students! At the end of the day, what matters most is that you love your costume. Happy Halloween!
