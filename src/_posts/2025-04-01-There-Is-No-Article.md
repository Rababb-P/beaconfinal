  Unfortunately, dear reader, I have some very troubling news for you. In the section of *The Beacon* that you are currently reading, there is actually no article! I am truly sorry for this inconvenience.  

    

Well, what are you waiting for? Scurry along now, like I said, there is no article here for you to read. Quite frankly, you are wasting your time by reading these meaningless words and sentences strung together to form a very obvious not-article. Why don’t you go read Ishani’s article about NTS Dramafest, I’m sure that’s very exciting. Ooh, or how about Kim’s article about how social media is ruining our lives? Doesn’t pique your interest? Fine, at least flip to the back of this paper and take a shot at Penny’s amazing crossword puzzle! You would be a fool to miss out on such quality entertainment.  

  
Why are you still reading this? How many times do I have to tell you, there is no article! I mean, it’s quite literally the title of this not-article! What exactly are you looking for, huh? Would you like… to hear a story? I have just the story for you! It’s called *The World’s Most Stubborn Beacon Reader*. Here’s how it goes:  

  
  Once upon a time, there was an MCI student who decided to pick up the latest issue of *The Beacon* as they walked through the front doors of the school. Unlike most other students who turn the corner and promptly chuck the paper straight into the recycling bin, this student decided to actually *read* the newspaper! But unfortunately, they got too caught up in reading a not-article, and they didn’t see the flock of geese waiting to violently attack them up ahead! The reader died after a period of excruciating pain (have you ever seen those freakishly sharp teeth-like tomia they have? Look it up, it’s horrifying). The end! Did you like the story? I thought you would. The main character reminds me of you for some reason…  

      

Okay fine, you wanna know the truth? There actually is an article. The real article… was the friends we made along the way. You see, I’m just a formless, incorporeal, anonymous narrator. And… I’ve been feeling so lonely recently. I just wanted somebody to talk to…
